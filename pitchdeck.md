## Slide 2 — The Problem

Companies struggle to collect feedback that is:

- **Fast** (too many tools, too much setup)
- **Honest** (fear of attribution, survey fatigue)
- **Actionable** (results are messy, no clear signal)
- **Shareable** (hard to publish results transparently)
- **Trackable** (no end-to-end analytics or response integrity)

**Impact:**

- Slower decisions, misaligned teams, low engagement
- Repeated meetings instead of clear data
- Lost institutional knowledge (“we asked once… then forgot”)

---

## Slide 3 — Why Existing Solutions Fail

Most tools are either:

- **Too heavy** (enterprise survey suites: slow setup, overkill)
- **Too shallow** (simple forms: no live results, weak analytics)
- **Not trust-first** (unclear anonymity controls, friction to respond)
- **Not designed for sharing** (no public links, hard to publish outcomes)

What teams need: **a feedback loop that feels effortless** but produces decision-grade data.

---

## Slide 4 — The Solution

**Narad Sabha** is a modern polling + insight platform that helps teams:

- Create polls in minutes
- Collect responses via a simple public link
- Choose **anonymous vs authenticated** participation
- Show **live results** when appropriate
- Analyze results per-question and at poll level
- Export response data when needed

**Outcome:** Faster alignment, higher participation, clearer decisions.

---

## Slide 5 — Product Overview (What Users Do)

**Creators (teams/leaders)**

- Build a poll (title, description, category, tags, expiry)
- Configure response rules (anonymous/auth, live results)
- Publish and share via link
- View analytics and individual responses
- Export responses (CSV)

**Respondents**

- Open link instantly
- Answer quickly on any device
- Submit with minimal friction

---

## Slide 6 — Core Features (Differentiators)

- **Anonymity controls**: choose anonymous or authenticated responses
- **Live results**: enable real-time transparency when needed
- **Poll lifecycle**: draft/active/closed/expired/published
- **Analytics built-in**: per-question counts + percentages, completion rate
- **Duplicate poll**: reuse successful templates
- **Export to CSV**: connect insights to your workflows
- **Rate limiting for anonymous submissions** (reduces spam/abuse)
- **Real-time updates via WebSockets** (live analytics refresh)
